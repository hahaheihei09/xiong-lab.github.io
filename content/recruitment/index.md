## Join us: postdoctoral research in agricultural robotics

The Strawberry Harvesting Robotics Group welcomes postdoctoral applications from China and abroad. Based at the Intelligent Equipment Research Center of the Beijing Academy of Agriculture and Forestry Sciences (BAAFS), we work on strawberry harvesting, laser weeding, and intelligent equipment for open-field vegetable production. We welcome researchers who want to connect algorithms and robotic systems with real agricultural settings.

### Research areas

- Robot vision, artificial intelligence, and foundation models for agriculture;
- Mechanism design, motion planning, and control for harvesting and weeding robots;

### Who can apply

Applicants should hold a PhD in robotics, AI, computer science, automation, mechanical engineering, or a related field; be able to conduct independent research and collaborate in a team; and be available for full-time postdoctoral research. We seek **highly motivated candidates** who are passionate about these research areas, want to solve real agricultural robotics challenges, and are eager to help bring research into practical use and commercialization. Formal admission, appointment category, and assessment requirements follow the current BAAFS rules.

International applicants are welcome. For foreign nationals interested in Beijing's international postdoctoral recruitment funding, we particularly encourage candidates who are generally **under 35**, have a relevant science or engineering PhD, and graduated from a university in the **top 100** of a major global overall or subject ranking for the applicable year. Eligibility and how rankings apply are determined by the current Beijing funding rules and approval process.

### Support and career development

- Compensation follows BAAFS postdoctoral policies. Its 2026 recruitment notice lists a **base annual salary of CNY 200,000–300,000**. National and Beijing funding may be combined where permitted; actual compensation depends on the appointment category, approved awards, and performance.
- Eligible foreign postdocs who are awarded Beijing international recruitment funding may receive additional project support. The program has **previously funded recipients at CNY 500,000 per person per year**. The current amount, duration, and whether it can be added to base compensation are subject to the year's rules, approval, and BAAFS confirmation.
- Eligible postdocs may apply for BAAFS postdoctoral housing. Where a qualified international postdoc is offered accommodation at a public-rental-housing rate, availability, rent, and eligibility are subject to BAAFS confirmation.
- Annual performance payments reflect research output, project contributions, and technology transfer. Strong commercialization outcomes may lead to higher performance payments, subject to institutional and team rules.
- Outstanding postdocs may receive priority consideration for a permanent position after completing their appointment, subject to vacancies and BAAFS recruitment procedures.

### Apply

Please email your CV, selected research outputs, and a brief research plan to [caucoexy@hotmail.com](mailto:caucoexy@hotmail.com), using “Agricultural Robotics Postdoc Application — Your Name” as the subject. We welcome an initial conversation about research fit.
