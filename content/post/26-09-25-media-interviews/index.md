In recent weeks, our team has been interviewed by several Chinese and international media outlets about our strawberry harvesting robot and our work on foundation models for open-field vegetable production.

The coverage reflects growing public interest in how artificial intelligence and robotics can address real agricultural challenges. Reports highlighted our work on fruit recognition and localization, active obstacle clearing, low-damage harvesting, autonomous navigation, and dual-arm coordination.

The People's Daily Overseas Edition introduced our fully autonomous strawberry harvesting robot and intelligent harvesting solution as an example of technology empowering primary industry. It highlighted integrated capabilities including fruit recognition and localization, active obstacle clearing, low-damage picking, autonomous navigation, and dual-arm coordination.

We appreciate the journalists and media organizations that took the time to learn about our work. For our team, media attention is also a reminder to keep research grounded in production needs. We will continue improving the reliability, efficiency, and affordability of agricultural robots and translating research results into practical tools for growers.
